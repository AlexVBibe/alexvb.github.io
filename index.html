1 Introduction

IronSet is a high-performance, data-driven fitness and nutrition ecosystem designed for athletes who demand precision. By integrating advanced analytics, Google Gemini AI, and real-time coaching via the "Gym Co-Pilot," IronSet transforms raw data into actionable training intelligence.

2 Getting Started

2.1 Profile Configuration

Navigate to the Setup widget to initialize your profile. Accurate data is critical for IronSet's calorie and performance algorithms:

  • Body Metrics — Input your Gender, Weight (kg), Age, and Height (cm).
  • Activity Mode:
    • IronSet High Intensity — High-intensity focused calculations.
    • Decent Standard — Standard activity level modeling.
  • Smart Watch Integration — Toggle "Enable Smart Watch" to sync real-time data with your Wear OS device.

2.2 AI Integration (Google Gemini)

To unlock AI Insights, you must provide a Google Gemini API Key:

  1. Tap "Get" in the Gemini API Key section to visit the Google AI Studio.
  2. Generate a key and paste it into the IronSet settings.
  3. This enables deep analysis of your training history and personalized workout recommendations.

3 Training & Workout Intelligence

3.1 The "Today" Dashboard

Your central hub for daily training.

  • Workout Selection: Choose from your active workout routines.
  • Workout Score: IronSet evaluates every routine based on:
    • Readiness — Your physical preparedness based on recent fatigue and recovery.
    • Progression — The mathematical opportunity to increase volume or intensity.
    • Priority — How the workout aligns with your weekly schedule.
  • Recommendations: If a workout score exceeds 1.0 Recommended, the Bar Chart icon will become active, offering an AI-optimized session plan.

3.2 Gym Co-Pilot (Autopilot)

The Gym Co-Pilot is your real-time coach during a session.

  • Guided Execution: It walks you through every set, including Warm-up · Yellow and Working · Red sets.
  • Session Management:
    • Confirm — Validates a successful set.
    • Failed — Triggers a failure report (input actual reps managed).
    • Later (Defer) — Moves the current exercise to the end of the session.
    • Skip — Skips the current set or exercise.
  • Persistence: If you pause a session, you can resume it later that day. If left overnight, the Co-Pilot will automatically finalize the data to maintain history integrity.

3.3 Active Tracking

Use the Track widget for manual set recording.

  • Visual Feedback: Real-time charts compare your "Today" performance against your "Last Session" and "Expected Volume."

4 Nutrition & Meal Planning

4.1 Planned Intake

Customize your daily nutritional targets in the Plan Meal widget:

  • Dynamic Sliders: Adjust Active Calories, Total Calories, Protein, Carbs, and Fats.
  • Objectives: Set your goal (e.g., Lean Bulk, Maintenance, Cutting) to let the engine suggest macro-nutrient distributions.
  • Calorie Calculator: Automatically estimates total expenditure based on your body metrics and planned activity level.

4.2 Meal History

The Meal History widget provides a weekly grid view of your caloric intake, allowing for easy navigation between past and current weeks to ensure consistency.

5 Advanced Analytics

Visualize your progress in the Analytics widget:

  • Multi-Series Charts: Overlay metrics like "Working Weight," "1RM (One Rep Max)," and "Total Volume."
  • Chart Types: Toggle between Line Charts for trend analysis and Bar Charts for set-by-set comparisons.
  • Time Scales: Use the navigation arrows to view data from the last 7 days to several months.

6 Hands-Free & Wear OS

6.1 Tilt Interaction

For a truly hands-free experience, enable Tilt Interaction in Settings:

  • Navigation: Scroll through widgets and charts by tilting your device.
  • Tracking: In the Track widget, tilt your device to adjust "Reps" without touching the screen.
  • Calibration: Hold the device at a comfortable angle and use the calibration tool to set the "zero" point.

6.2 Wear OS Sync

  • IronSet bi-directionally syncs with your Wear OS watch.
  • Check "Watch Status" in Setup to ensure your device is online.
  • Sync history logs provide transparency on data transfers.

7 Data Management

Your data is stored locally for privacy.

  • Backup: Create a timestamped backup of your history and settings.
  • Restore: Revert to a previous state using the available backup list.
  • Delete All: A secure way to wipe all workout history, meal data, and profile settings.
🔒
Privacy by design. All data lives in an application-private location on your device. Nothing leaves your phone except when you explicitly request AI Insights or Food Scanning, which send the relevant data to Google Gemini for analysis.

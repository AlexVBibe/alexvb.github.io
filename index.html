IronSet: Training & Nutrition Manual

1. Introduction

IronSet is a high-performance, data-driven fitness and nutrition ecosystem designed for athletes and individuals who demand precision and control. By integrating advanced analytics, Google Gemini AI, and real-time coaching via the "Gym Co-Pilot," IronSet transforms raw data into actionable training intelligence. The application does not share your data with anyone; everything is stored inside application private location on your device and controlled by OS and your mobile phone security features. Some functionality like getting Ai insight about your training may sent a request to Gemini Ai to analyse the training session to give you recommendations, apart from that nothing is shared or sent somewhere.

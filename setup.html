Profile Configuration

Navigate to the Setup widget to initialize your profile. Accurate data is critical for IronSet’s calorie and performance algorithms: • Body Metrics: o Input your Gender, o Weight (kg), o Age, and o Height (cm). • Activity Mode (used in calibrating your daily intake, mostly affects ratio between Carbs/Protein/Fat: o IronSet: High-intensity focused calculations. o Decent: Standard activity level modelling. • Smart Watch Integration: o Toggle "Enable Smart Watch" to sync real-time data with your Wear OS device.

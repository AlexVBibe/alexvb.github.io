3. Training & Workout Intelligence

How IronSet guides, scores, and tracks every training session.

3.1 The "Today" Dashboard

Your central hub for daily training.

  • Workout Selection: Choose from your active workout routines.
  • Workout Score: IronSet evaluates every routine based on:
    • Readiness — Your physical preparedness based on recent fatigue and recovery.
    • Progression — The mathematical opportunity to increase volume or intensity.
    • Priority — How the workout aligns with your weekly schedule.
  • Recommendations: If a workout score exceeds 1.0, the Bar Chart icon will become active, offering an AI-optimized session plan.
📊 Today Dashboard — screen preview

The screen is designed to start and track your workout session. Here, you can find all the workouts and exercises you selected on the Setup screen. Select the workout or exercise you want to perform and start tracking your progress.

3.2 Gym Co-Pilot (Autopilot)

The Gym Co-Pilot is your real-time buddy during a session.

Guided Execution

It walks you through every set, including Warm-up Sets · Yellow and Working Sets · Red

Session Management

  • Confirm — Validates a successful set.
  • Failed — Triggers a failure report (input actual reps managed).
  • Later (Defer) — Moves the current exercise to the end of the session.
  • Skip — Skips the current set or exercise.

Persistence

If you pause a session, you can resume it later that day. If left overnight, the Co-Pilot will automatically finalize the data to maintain history integrity.

3.3 Active Tracking

Use the Track widget for manual set recording.

Visual Feedback

Real-time charts compare your "Today" performance against your "Last Session" and "Expected Volume."

Performance Metrics

Performance Index
A percentage reflecting how well you performed against your estimated capabilities.
Fatigue Level
Tracks the drop in power output across sets.
Intensity
A color-coded indicator warning you when intensity levels are suboptimal or excessive.
